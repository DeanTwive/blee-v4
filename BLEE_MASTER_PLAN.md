## THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE STRATEGY

### Why Identity Beats Motivation

James Clear (Atomic Habits, 2018): "The most durable behavior change comes from identity, not outcome." Someone who says 'I am a runner' keeps running when motivation fails.

Every design decision in Blee reinforces the user's identity as a runner above all else.

### The 66-Day Formation Window (Lally et al., 2010)

Habit formation takes an average of 66 days, not 21.

- Weeks 1-2: Novelty phase, highest churn risk
- Weeks 3-9: The Formation Window, critical investment period
- Weeks 10+: Automaticity, habit is self-sustaining

Strategic implication: Maximum product investment during weeks 3-9. Not at launch. During the window.

### Self-Determination Theory (Deci and Ryan, 1985)

| Need | Meaning | Blee Feature |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Autonomy | "I choose this" | Self-declared tiers, opt-in comparisons |
| Competence | "I am getting better" | Cadence coach, RPE tracking, analytics |
| Relatedness | "I belong" | Groups, buddy matching, community feed |

Every feature must satisfy at least one SDT need.

### The Peak-End Rule (Kahneman and Fredrickson, 1993)

People remember experiences by their peak moment and their ending, not the average.

- The Peak: Blee detects fastest km, highest elevation, or longest effort and surfaces it on the card
- The End: The Run Receipt reveal IS the ending of every run experience

Design rule: Invest more in the Run Receipt reveal animation than any other animation in the app.

## PART 1 — THE RUNNER

### 1.1 Identity System — Fully Decoupled

Two independent constructs that never interfere with each other.

**A. Identity Tier — Self-Declared, Always Fluid**

| Tier | Label | SDT Primary Need | I run because... |
|------|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| Green | Pacer | Relatedness + Autonomy | I want to feel better and enjoy movement |
| Blue | Strider | Competence + Relatedness | I want to track progress and reach new goals |
| Red | Elite | Competence + Autonomy | I want to perform, race, and push my limits |

Rules:
- Selected at onboarding (one tap, framed as "Why do you run?" not "How fast are you?")
- Changeable anytime, zero friction, zero penalty
- Never displayed as a rank — shown as running personality on profile
- Zero performance gates — a 10:00/km runner can be an Elite; a 4:00/km runner can be a Pacer

**B. Verified Badges — Optional, Performance-Earned, Available to All Tiers**

| Badge | How Earned | Who Can Earn |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Century Runner | 100 logged runs on Blee | Any tier |
| Half Marathon Club | One run 21.1km or more | Any tier |
| Race Finisher | Race bib OCR upload (Phase 2) | Any tier |
| Club Leader | 10+ group runs organized | Any tier |
| Coach | Training plan used by 5+ others | Any tier |

A Pacer can earn every badge. An Elite earns no badge automatically. Parallel tracks, not hierarchy.

### 3.3 GPS Background Tracking — Technical Foundation

Solve GPS reliability first. Everything else depends on it.

Android Battery Kill Mitigation:
1. Foreground Service with persistent non-dismissible notification: "Blee recording — 3.2km"
2. Wake Lock (PARTIAL_WAKE_LOCK) prevents CPU sleep during tracking
3. Onboarding battery screen with device-specific instructions (Screen 6)
4. GPS health check: after runs 1-3, detect gaps over 30 seconds and prompt fix
5. Help center links to dontkillmyapp.com for community-maintained OEM instructions

iOS: UIBackgroundModes set to location, allowsBackgroundLocationUpdates true, pausesLocationUpdatesAutomatically false.

GPS Data Architecture:
- Record lat/lng every 5 seconds (not 3 — 40% battery reduction, minimal accuracy loss)
- Store to on-device SQLite during run (zero network dependency)
- On completion: batch-upload single PostGIS LINESTRING to Supabase (1 insert per run)
- Idempotency key prevents duplicate uploads; unique constraint on user_id + started_at

Why Supabase PostGIS over Firebase RTDB:
Firebase = 720 writes per 60-min run per user. At 10,000 concurrent = 7.2M writes/hour. Unpredictable billing.
Supabase = 1 insert per completed run. Flat, predictable, enables geospatial queries.

### 3.7 Safety Architecture

LIVE BEACON — CORRECTLY SCOPED

What it is: A location-sharing tool for peace of mind.
What it is not: An emergency response service.

Stated in onboarding, in-feature UI, and Terms of Service:
"Blee Live Beacon shares your location with trusted contacts while you run.
It is not an emergency service. In any emergency, call 911 immediately."

Live Beacon v1 — WhatsApp/iMessage Native (Zero Cost):
1. Tap Live Beacon, select contacts from address book
2. Blee generates blee.run/live/{32-char-random-token} (HTTPS only, Cloudflare-protected)
3. Native share sheet — WhatsApp, iMessage, Telegram (not SMS by default)
4. Contact sees live location in browser, updates every 30 seconds, no app required
5. Link auto-expires 2 hours after run end, or on manual stop

Endpoint Security:
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Token guessing | 32-char cryptographic random token (256-bit entropy) |
| DDoS attack | Cloudflare rate limiting: max 10 requests per minute per IP |
| Location scraping | Supabase RLS: token-authenticated reads only |
| Stale data | Last-update timestamp shown to contact |

Protocol:
| Scenario | Behavior |
|----------|----------|
| Internet lost mid-run | Location resumes on reconnect; last known location shown |
| Phone battery dies | Last known location plus timestamp; no false arrival message |
| Link expiry during long run | Auto-extends if run still active, max 4 hours |
| Manual stop | Instant token invalidation |
| Run auto-detected complete | "Dean finished safely. 5.3km" then token expires 30 minutes later |

Community Moderation:
| Layer | Tool | Response |
|-------|------|---------|
| AI moderation | Google Cloud Natural Language API | Real-time |
| Keyword filter | Custom list (pace-shaming, body commentary) | Real-time |
| Human review | Community manager | Under 24 hours |
| Report | 3 taps: Report, Category, Submit | Immediate acknowledgment |
| Block | Bidirectional, instant | Immediate |
| Group admin | Remove, approve, pin rules | Self-serve |

## PART 5 — BEHAVIORAL PSYCHOLOGY

### 5.1 The Four-Framework Stack

| Framework | Application in Blee |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Hook Model (Nir Eyal) | Trigger, Action, Variable Reward, Investment |
| Self-Determination Theory (Deci and Ryan, 1985) | Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness applied per tier |
| Peak-End Rule (Kahneman and Fredrickson, 1993) | Run Receipt reveal = the memorable ending of every run |
| 66-Day Formation Window (Lally et al., 2010) | Maximum product investment in weeks 3-9 |

---

### 5.2 Hook Model — Encouragement-First

| Component | Guilt Version | Encouragement Version |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------|
| External Trigger | "Your group ran without you" | "Saturday's BGC run is in 2 days. Sarah's going. Join them?" |
| Internal Trigger | Streak anxiety, guilt | Excitement for group; curiosity about stats |
| Action | Open from guilt | Open for community, feel motivated, run |
| Variable Reward | Badge? PR? | Group cheered your run! Peak moment detected! |
| Investment | Build streak | Log mood, write quote, invite friend, plan next run |

---

### 5.3 Motivation by Tier — SDT Applied

PACERS — Relatedness + Autonomy

Core fear: Judgment, comparison, "I don't belong."

Blee solutions:
- Feed default: Pacer-tier runs only for first 30 days (eliminates intimidating pace data)
- Zero-judgment policy: pace-negative comments removed in under 1 hour
- Effort celebration: "9/10 effort — your hardest session yet." Never references pace
- Buddy match: opt-in only, double consent, easy exit
- Progression invite at 20 runs: gentle peek at Strider content, user chooses

66-day strategy: Weeks 3-9 get maximum community investment — buddy check-ins, group run invites.
Formation Window badge earned at 10-week mark if 2+ runs per week sustained.

STRIDERS — Competence + Relatedness

Core fear: Plateau, "Am I even improving?"

Blee solutions:
- Plateau detection: 3-week stagnation — offer (not alarm): "Want to try a tempo run?"
- Weekly self-comparison: self vs last week only
- Group collective stats: "Your club ran 287km combined. You contributed 23km."
- Opt-in effort leaderboard: runs per week, tier-restricted
- Strider Spotlight: monthly feature of one Strider's journey

ELITES — Competence + Autonomy

Core fear: Isolation, "No one is at my level here."

Blee solutions:
- Coach Mode (Phase 2): publish training plans, build a following, attribution maintained
- Verified performance badges: earned, not self-declared
- Elite-only discussion threads in groups
- Race result integration: bib OCR to official finish time on profile card
- Blee Top 100 annual: community contribution score (plans made, runs led), not pace

---

### 5.4 The 66-Day Formation Window — Product Strategy

Week 1-2 (Novelty, highest churn risk):
- Onboarding, first run, Run Receipt, share
- If no run in 48 hours: one gentle nudge ("Your first run is waiting"), then silence

Weeks 3-9 (Formation Window — Maximum Investment):
- Buddy check-in system active (if opted in)
- Personalized group run invitations timed to user's typical run time
- Cadence Coach unlocks (Phase 2)
- Formation Window badge: private progress tracker visible only to user
- Weekly personal summary delivered Sunday evening
- Missed week: one warm re-engagement message ("Breaks are part of training. Ready when you are."), never repeated

Week 10+ (Automaticity):
- Running becomes self-sustaining, less intervention needed
- Features shift from habit formation to performance improvement and community depth
- Premium features more valuable (user now has data and history to analyze)

---

### 5.5 Consistency Rhythm — Not Streaks

Why streaks injure runners: Daily streak mechanics force runs when the body needs rest — fastest path to overuse injury and permanent dropout.

Blee's alternative:
- Unit: active weeks, not consecutive days
- Active week: any 2 runs within a 7-day window
- Counter name: "Rhythm" — musical connotation, includes rests
- Rest weeks celebrated: "Recovery week. Smart. Your rhythm continues."
- Streak Shield: earned every 4 active weeks, protects against 1 missed week
- Comeback Protocol: "Breaks are part of training. Ready when you are." Sent once, never repeated.

---

### 5.6 Social Comparison — SDT-Informed Balance

Always public: Run frequency, total distance, badges earned, tier (personality not rank).
Never public by default: Exact pace, heart rate, leaderboard rank, route details.
Opt-in tools: Friend Activity (effort + frequency only), City Leaderboard (km per month, tier-restricted), You vs Last Month (personal comparison primary).

Lateral comparison (peers at similar level) increases motivation.
Upward social comparison (seeing someone much faster) damages competence need for beginners.
Blee defaults to lateral or self-comparison. Upward comparison is opt-in only.

## APPENDIX B — THE INVESTOR PITCH

The problem: 80% of people who try running quit within 3 months. Not from lack of motivation — because they never felt they belonged. Strava built for the top 10% and left the other 90% feeling like outsiders.

The solution: Blee is the running community built for the 90%. We track effort, not pace. We build clubs, not leaderboards. Every run generates a beautiful "Run Receipt" card that spreads the app virally without a dollar spent on ads.

The business: Freemium individual at 8.99 per month. B2B clubs at 24.99 per month (no App Store fee, 2% churn). Brand partnerships. Club revenue anchors the model; individual subscriptions scale it.

The moat: When a running club migrates to Blee, their social graph, event history, and training plans migrate with them. Switching costs compound with community size. Strava cannot copy our culture without destroying theirs.

The market: 600 million recreational runners globally. Philippines: 10 million plus recreational runners, zero dominant community platform.

The ask: 400,000 USD pre-seed. 24-month runway. Milestone: 44,500 MAU and break-even by Month 28.
